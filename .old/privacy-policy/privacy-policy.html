Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2025

At Krish Satasiya's portfolio and service platform (https://krishsatasiya.netlify.app), your privacy and trust are paramount. This Privacy Policy outlines how I handle, use, and protect your data when you engage with my services—be it freelance, job-based, or via this website.

Information Collection & Usage

When you share information with me—whether it's through contact forms, emails, or during a freelance/job assignment—you can be assured that your data is used solely for completing the services you've requested. I may collect basic personal information such as your name, email address, and files relevant to the project. These are used only to communicate with you, fulfill service commitments, and ensure quality deliverables. I do not collect any sensitive personal details like Aadhaar numbers, banking credentials, or biometric information.

Data Retention & Deletion

Once a project is completed and delivered, your data—including any files, access credentials, assets, or documents—is stored for a period of up to 30 days. After this timeframe, all project-related information is permanently erased from my systems, including from local machines and cloud storage (if used). For this reason, no backups are maintained or provided beyond this period, and it is solely the client's responsibility to download and store their deliverables safely. I do not offer data recovery or post-deletion file access.

Confidentiality & Portfolio Usage

As a freelancer and developer working under contract or one-time projects, all shared content—whether it's private documentation, user databases, UI designs, or brand materials—is treated with the highest level of confidentiality. If you prefer to have a legally binding document in place, I'm open to signing a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before project commencement. Unless bound by an NDA, I may showcase the final output of publicly visible work (excluding private data or proprietary code) in my portfolio.

Project Agreements & Refund Policy

All freelance and job engagements are assumed to be governed by mutual understanding unless a formal contract exists. Without a signed contract, I shall not be held liable for disputes or modifications post-project delivery. Please note that unless otherwise stated in writing, all services rendered are non-refundable once the work has been initiated or completed.

Legal Compliance

To ensure ethical and legal compliance, this Privacy Policy adheres to applicable Indian laws, including the Information Technology Act, 2000, and upcoming provisions under the Personal Data Protection Bill. For U.S.-based clients or projects, I also comply with major federal privacy guidelines such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the Federal Trade Commission Act (FTC Act), and the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA). I follow global best practices in data minimization, usage limitation, and privacy protection across jurisdictions.

Third-Party Services

In some cases, third-party services (e.g., Google Drive, Firebase, GitHub, Figma) may be used to collaborate on or host project files. While I ensure secure usage from my end, users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of these external services as I cannot control how they manage your data.

Your Rights

As a user, you reserve the right to request early deletion of your data, inquire about what information is held (during the active project window), or revoke access at any time—keeping in mind that this may affect the service timeline or outcome. I do not knowingly collect information from users under the age of 18. If you're a minor, please do not share any personal information through the website.

Policy Updates

I may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in practices or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated date. By continuing to use this website or services, you accept the revised policy in full.
